## Featured Projects

### 1. Compound Dry Hazards Across the Pan-Asian Region
**Research Project | 1980–2021**

Investigated the spatial and temporal characteristics of compound drought, heatwave, fire danger, and extreme wind events across Asia.

**Key methods and contributions:**
- Processing and analysis of large-scale ERA5 climate datasets.
- Development of threshold-based compound hazard identification methods.
- Statistical trend analysis using Mann-Kendall and Sen's slope.
- Spatial and temporal assessment of compound hazard occurrences.
- Analysis of cascading events and hazard dependence structures.

**Technologies:** Python, R, Xarray, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, GIS.

### 2. Compound Heatwave, Drought, and Dry Air Events in Asia (2023)
**Ongoing Research**

Investigating the characteristics of compound heatwave, drought, and low-relative-humidity events across Asia, including their spatial and temporal variability, cumulative anomalies, and historical context.

**Research focus:**
- Climate extreme identification and characterization.
- Historical comparisons and anomaly analysis.
- Statistical assessment of event frequency and severity.
- Climate attribution and future time-of-emergence analysis.

**Technologies:** Python, R, climate datasets, statistical analysis, and geospatial visualization.
